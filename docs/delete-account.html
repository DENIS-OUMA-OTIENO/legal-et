Delete your PesaLens account

Developer: PesaLens · App: PesaLens (com.expensetracker.app)

You can delete your account and everything linked to it at any time. There are two ways.

Option 1: in the app (immediate)

  1. Open PesaLens and sign in.
  2. On the Dashboard, tap the gear icon in the top-right corner.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your cloud backup and your sign-in are deleted straight away, and the transactions stored on the phone are cleared. You are returned to the welcome screen.

Option 2: by email (if you no longer have the app)

Send a request from the Google account email you used to sign in, so we can verify it is you:

Email a deletion request

Subject: Delete my PesaLens account. No other details are needed. We confirm by reply and complete the deletion within 30 days, usually much sooner.

What is deleted

  • Your account (Firebase Authentication user).
  • All transactions backed up to our servers.
  • Categories you created.
  • When done in the app: the local database on that phone, including any stored SMS alert text. If you deleted by email instead, uninstall the app to remove local data.

What is kept

  • Nothing tied to you. Residual copies in server backups are purged within 30 days, and routine server logs expire within 30 days.
Deletion cannot be undone. If you reinstall PesaLens later and sign in with the same Google account, you start with an empty history.
