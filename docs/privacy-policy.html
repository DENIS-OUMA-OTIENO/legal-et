PesaLens Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026 · Applies to the PesaLens Android app (package com.expensetracker.app)

PesaLens turns the mobile-money SMS alerts already on your phone into categorised expenses. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is stored, and how you can delete it. It is written to be read in a few minutes; if anything is unclear, email us at support.

In one paragraph: SMS alerts are read and parsed on your phone. The text of your messages is never uploaded. Only the extracted transaction details (amount, merchant, category, date, reference) are backed up to your account so you can restore them on another device. We show no ads, use no tracking SDKs and never sell your data. You can delete your account and every record we hold from inside the app.

2. The SMS permission

PesaLens requests the Android RECEIVE_SMS and READ_SMS permissions for a single purpose: turning mobile-money transaction alerts (for example M-Pesa "Confirmed…" messages) into expenses automatically.

  • RECEIVE_SMS detects alerts as they arrive, including while the app is closed, so each payment becomes an expense without typing.
  • READ_SMS is used once, when you set up a new account that has no history to restore: the app looks up the messages from the M-Pesa sender in your inbox and imports your five most recent payments so your dashboard does not start empty. It does not scan the rest of your inbox and never imports your full message history.
  • Every message the app looks at is checked on the device. Messages that are not a mobile-money transaction alert are ignored and never stored or transmitted.
  • The text of a matching alert stays in the app's private storage on your phone. It is never uploaded to our servers or shared with anyone.
  • Our use of SMS data follows Google Play's SMS and Call Log permissions policy under the "SMS-based money management" exception. SMS data is used only to provide this feature. It is not used for advertising, analytics, profiling, or any other purpose, and it is not sold or shared.
  • You can withdraw the permission at any time in Android Settings → Apps → PesaLens → Permissions. The app keeps working; you simply stop getting automatic capture.

3. How we use your information

  • Provide the app: capture expenses, categorise them, show lists and monthly insights.
  • Back up your transactions to your account and restore them when you sign in on another phone or reinstall.
  • Keep your account secure and prevent abuse.
  • Respond to support requests you send us.

We do not show advertising, do not use advertising or analytics SDKs, do not build profiles for marketing, and do not sell or rent personal information.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Cloud data is kept for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account it is removed immediately, and any residual copies in backups are purged within 30 days.
  • On-device data (including matched SMS text) is removed when you delete your account in the app or uninstall it. The app opts out of Android auto-backup, so its database is not copied to your Google Drive backups.
  • Server logs are kept for up to 30 days.

To delete your account and all associated data: open PesaLens → tap the gear icon on the Dashboard → Delete account. You can also use our account deletion page if you no longer have the app installed.

6. Security

All traffic between the app and our services uses HTTPS. Access to your backup requires a valid Firebase sign-in token. On the device, data lives in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your phone and Google account protected.

7. Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your information at any time from inside the app or by contacting us. If you are in Kenya, you also have the rights set out in the Data Protection Act, 2019; if you are elsewhere, local data-protection law may give you similar rights. We respond to requests within 30 days.

8. Children

PesaLens is a personal-finance tool intended for adults (18 and over). We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information we will update this page and the effective date above, and, for material changes, tell you in the app.
